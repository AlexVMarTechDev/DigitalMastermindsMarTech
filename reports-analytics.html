How We Report

Signal, decision, action, compound.

Reporting is only useful if it changes what happens next. Each week follows the same loop.

Signal

Every call, form, chat, text, and booking captured in one place.

Decision

A weekly review of what changed and why, in plain language.

Action

Budgets, messages, and agent behavior adjusted against the findings.

Compound

What worked is kept and extended. What didn't is retired.

What's In The Report

Outcomes, not vanity metrics.

Lead sources & response time

Where inquiries come from and how quickly each one received a first reply.

Bookings & show rate

Appointments booked from each source and how many actually happened.

Reviews & reputation

Review volume, rating, and reply time across the places customers look.

Agent activity

What the agents handled, what they escalated to your team, and what changed as a result.

Spend vs. outcome

Marketing spend set against booked business, so budget moves follow results.
