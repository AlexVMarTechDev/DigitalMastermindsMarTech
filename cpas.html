FAQ

Questions from CPA firm owners.

How can a CPA firm win more clients outside of tax season?

Stay visible all year and make it easy to start. DMM plans local search, advisory-focused content, email and referral campaigns so prospective clients find you before deadlines hit, then adds agents that answer questions and onboard them. Your diagnosis shows where inquiries arrive and where they stall.

Can an AI agent chase client documents for us?

Yes. A document-chasing agent sends polite, timed reminders for missing items, answers simple status questions, and tells your staff who is still outstanding. Your team decides what is needed and reviews anything that affects a filing.

Does the agent give tax advice?

No. Agents draft, track and follow up. They never give tax or filing advice, and anything requiring professional judgment is escalated to your team, so clients always hear from a qualified person on those questions.

Who is responsible if an agent gets something wrong?

Your firm remains responsible, so every agent output is designed to be reviewed by your team before it reaches a client or a filing. People stay in control of every decision that carries liability.

How is client data protected?

Agents work only with the information your firm chooses to connect, inside your existing systems. We design data handling around your confidentiality obligations and confirm it with you before launch, and your firm decides what is never shared.

Can it connect to our practice management and document portal?

In most cases, yes. The Corestack connects to the practice management and document tools most firms already run. We confirm compatibility during your diagnosis, before any build starts.

What does it cost to work with DMM?

We quote after your diagnosis, because cost depends on your client roster and tools. AI agents come in three tiers, Foundation, Growth and Full Journey, each with a one-time setup fee and a monthly retainer. Marketing services are scoped separately.
