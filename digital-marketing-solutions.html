Search & AI Visibility

Be found in search, and be named in the answer.

More people now ask Google, voice assistants, and AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity who to call before they ever open a website. Our search work covers all three layers, so your business is easy to find, easy to quote, and easy to recommend.

SEO

Rank where people search

Search Engine Optimization earns steady traffic from Google and Bing, and from the map results nearby customers rely on.

  • Technical fixes and site speed
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile, listings, reviews
  • Pages built around the questions customers ask
AEO

Be the direct answer

Answer Engine Optimization shapes your content so search features and assistants can lift a clear answer straight from your site.

  • Question-and-answer pages in plain language
  • Structured data that matches what visitors read
  • Featured snippets, AI Overviews, and voice answers
GEO

Be cited by AI tools

Generative Engine Optimization builds the trust signals AI tools weigh when they recommend a business in a conversation.

  • Consistent business facts across your site, listings, and directories
  • Original, expert content worth quoting
  • Regular checks on how AI tools describe you
Honest expectations

No one controls what a search engine or an AI tool says, and we do not promise rankings or citations. We strengthen the signals that make your business easy to find, trust, and quote, then measure where you show up. People who arrive from an AI answer are ready to ask, so the Lead Response Agent is there to reply in minutes.
