About DMM

What does Digital Masterminds (DMM) do?

DMM is a full-service digital marketing agency with generative and agentic AI built in. We start with a diagnosis of where your business stands, then plan advertising, SEO, AI search visibility (AEO and GEO), creative, email and text campaigns, and AI agents together across the whole customer journey, from the first click to the returning client.

What are AEO and GEO, and does DMM do them?

Yes. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) makes your content the direct answer in search features and voice assistants. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) builds the signals AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity use when recommending a business. We plan both alongside SEO and never promise a ranking or citation.

Who does DMM work with?

Service businesses that win clients through inquiries, appointments and repeat visits: MedSpas, health and wellness brands, real estate teams, law firms, CPA firms, architects, contractors and home service companies, and multi-location brands. Each industry gets its own plan and its own review steps.

What is the DMM Diagnosis?

It is a clear picture of your business before we recommend anything: how potential clients find you, where they drop off, how often current clients come back, and how nearby competitors compare. Its depth grows with the plan you choose, so it is part of working with us, not a free full audit.

AI agents and your team

Will AI agents replace my team?

No. Agents handle the repeatable first steps, such as replying, booking and following up, so your team can focus on the service, the judgment calls and the relationships. People stay in charge of every decision that matters.

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent also takes action: it books the appointment, updates the record, sends the follow-up and reports what it did. Agents talk with people naturally by chat, text or voice, and every agent has a review point where your team can step in.

Which tools do the AI agents work with?

Agents connect to the scheduling, CRM and messaging tools you already use. We map your current setup during your diagnosis and confirm compatibility before any build starts, so you aren't asked to replace systems that already work.

Cost and getting started

How much does DMM cost?

We quote after your diagnosis, because cost depends on your goals, volume and tools. AI agent plans come in three tiers, Foundation, Growth and Full Journey, each with a one-time setup fee and a monthly retainer. Marketing services are scoped to the channels you actually need.

How long until we see results?

It depends on the channel. Advertising, chat and agent follow-up can show early signals sooner, while SEO, AEO, GEO and content build over months. We agree on what to measure up front and report on it regularly, so you see progress along the way.

How do I get started?

Book a call or send a short note about your business. We talk through your goals, run your DMM Diagnosis, and come back with a plan sized to your investment. You decide what moves forward.

Does DMM work with businesses outside New Jersey?

Yes. DMM is based in Montclair, New Jersey, and works with service businesses in New Jersey and beyond. Our diagnosis, campaigns and agents are delivered remotely, with calls and video meetings at times that suit your team.
